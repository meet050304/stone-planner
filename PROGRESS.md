## Working in the viewer

- 3D and scan image views, aligned to the scan; damaged frames removed; smooth drag and zoom.
- Tab shows or hides inclusions, D hides the diamond, Shift-drag moves the view.
- Pick inclusions: green selected, yellow ignored. Picks are remembered per stone.
- Keys 1 to 5 fit that many stones, 0 returns to Advisor's plan. Fitting uses the cut limits.
- Details panel with editable proportions, and grading limits under Cut limits.
- Saw plate: 25 um by default, drawn between stones. Edit saw rotates (left-drag) and moves (right-drag), then refits.
- Split inclusion: draw a line to divide an inclusion into two parts.
- Click a polished stone to see it alone. Tab shows the inclusions inside it, in the same three colours.
- Safety distance: 10 um by default, under Cut limits.
- Auto select follows the INCLUSION LIST, size being the diagonal across the two longest box sides: all Use, 0-201 um Ignore, 202 um to the higher of CrownInc and TableInc (list CROWN+ less 30, TABLE CTR+ plus 1, by rough weight) Auto, up to TableInc allowed under the table, above it under the crown only, wholly within 100 um of the surface back to Use, drop pin marks Ignore. Matches 88.7% of 465,060 inclusions on 195 untouched Advisor stones. Steps are editable under Cut limits. Auto inclusions may stay inside stones.
- Auto plan: tries 1 to 5 stones and keeps the highest profit (22,320 per carat up to 1.25 mm, 14,415 above, 60 labour per stone, 3% polishing loss, sizes 1.26 to 1.33 mm blocked). Prices are under Cut limits.
- Export CSV: writes `[Stone Name].csv` with only the columns and rows of Advisor's export, and Charni from Advisor's SieveSize.xml, for the bulk data software, also on every Save .adv. Export folder picks where it goes, otherwise Downloads.
- Bulk plan: reads every `.adv` in the Stone folder without changing it, runs Auto plan with either the file's saved inclusions or Auto select, and writes results to a separate Output folder with a CSV per stone and `bulk-compare.csv`. Shows a profit comparison with the old plan.
- Unprocessed stones (no plan in the file) open with their rough and inclusions, and work with Auto plan and Bulk plan. Their plan goes to the plan file and CSV.
- Save plan and Open plan use a `.plan.json` file. Save .adv overwrites the stone's file in the Stone folder with no backup and updates its summary values, then writes the CSV to the Export folder. Both folders are remembered.
